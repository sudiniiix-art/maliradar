MaliRadar Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 • Effective October 7, 2026

MaliRadar is a paper-trading and market-learning simulator. This Privacy Policy explains what information MaliRadar may store or transmit, why it is used, who may receive it, how it is protected, and the choices available to you.

Developer / publisher: MaliRadar. Before Google Play publication, the developer identity shown on the Play listing should match the developer identity used in this policy.

Privacy contact: You may submit a privacy question or deletion request through the MaliRadar repository issue mechanism: Privacy request.

1. Information MaliRadar handles

Information stored on your device

  • Paper-trading cash, holdings, orders and simulator history.
  • Watchlists, alerts, market/region preferences, learning progress, tutorial status and appearance settings.
  • Your optional local profile information, such as a chosen username and avatar.
  • Policy acceptance records, including the accepted policy version and acceptance time.

This information is primarily stored in your device/browser storage. Clearing app data, clearing site storage, or using the app's reset/delete controls can remove local information.

Information sent to MaliRadar services

Some optional social and online features transmit information away from your device. When you use them, MaliRadar may process:

  • A MaliRadar profile ID generated for the profile.
  • Your chosen username and optional profile image/avatar.
  • Your selected region/market.
  • Learning XP, simulated profit/return, paper-trading activity and achievement statistics used for leaderboards and challenges.
  • Friend requests and friend relationships when you use the Friends features.
  • Paper-order or alert information only when a particular online feature explicitly sends it to the server.

Push notifications

If push alerts are enabled, the app may transmit a client/device identifier, push subscription information and the active alert settings required to deliver the notifications. Push notifications are optional.

Market and news data

MaliRadar requests market quotes, historical market information and news through server-side or third-party providers. Your search terms, selected market or requested symbol may be sent as necessary to obtain the requested information. Provider systems may have their own privacy policies and retention practices.

Information we do not intentionally request for the current app

MaliRadar does not ask you to provide a password, bank-account password, payment-card number, brokerage login or real-money trading credential. Do not enter such information into free-text fields or any feature that is not explicitly designed for it.

2. Why information is used

  • To operate the paper-trading simulator and save your preferences.
  • To provide optional leaderboards, challenges, profiles and Friends features.
  • To provide optional alerts and notifications.
  • To provide requested market and news information.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, diagnose technical problems and keep the service reliable.
  • To display learning and simulated-performance statistics inside MaliRadar.

MaliRadar does not use simulated trading results as a promise of future financial performance.

3. Sharing and third parties

MaliRadar may share or transmit information with service providers needed to operate features you use, such as market-data/news providers, hosting/infrastructure providers and notification services. MaliRadar does not sell your personal information.

Public competition features may display information such as your chosen username, profile/avatar, region and simulated statistics to other users. Do not put private or sensitive information in a username, avatar or public profile.

4. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the type of information handled. Data sent between the app and our server is intended to use HTTPS/TLS when the service is deployed over HTTPS. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so avoid submitting information that MaliRadar does not need.

5. Retention

Local simulator and preference data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the app, clear app/site storage, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove the stored data. Online profile, social and competition information may be retained while the associated profile is active and for a reasonable period needed for backups, security, dispute handling or legal obligations.

When an online profile deletion request is received, MaliRadar will remove or anonymize associated profile and social records within a reasonable operational period, subject to information that must be retained for legal or security reasons.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • You can use MaliRadar's local delete/reset controls to remove local profile and simulator information.
  • You can disable optional push alerts through the app's notification controls.
  • You can request deletion of an online MaliRadar profile or privacy information by using the privacy request mechanism.
  • You can request access or correction of information associated with an online profile through the same privacy mechanism.

7. Children's privacy

MaliRadar is a general market-learning application and is not designed as a children's service. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating a real-money financial account. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us through the privacy request mechanism so we can review and take appropriate action.

8. Financial education disclaimer

MaliRadar is a simulator and educational tool. Paper results are hypothetical. Market data can be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Smart Assist and other analytical features are educational classifications and do not constitute financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's data practices or legal requirements change. When a material change requires renewed acknowledgement, MaliRadar may show the policy acceptance screen again and record the new policy version.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, access/correction requests or deletion requests, use the MaliRadar privacy request mechanism.

Important: This policy is written to align the app's disclosures with Google Play's privacy-policy and Data safety expectations, but it is not a legal opinion. The final Play Data safety declarations must match the exact production build, SDKs, server configuration and data practices.
