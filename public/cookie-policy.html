STOCKCRASH Cookie Policy

Version 1.0 • Effective October 8, 2026

STOCKCRASH is a paper-trading and market-learning simulator. This Cookie Policy explains how STOCKCRASH uses cookies and similar browser technologies.

Developer / publisher: STOCKCRASH.

This Cookie Policy should be read together with the STOCKCRASH Privacy Policy.

1. Does STOCKCRASH use cookies?

STOCKCRASH does not currently intentionally use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies in the application.

The current web application primarily uses browser storage, including localStorage, to keep simulator state, preferences, learning progress, watchlists, alerts and other settings on the user's device. Browser storage is not the same technology as a traditional HTTP cookie, but it is treated as a similar technology for transparency purposes.

2. What similar technologies are used?

  • Local storage: Used to save paper-trading cash, holdings, transaction history, watchlists, alerts, learning progress, profile preferences, region settings, tutorial status and other app settings.
  • Session/runtime storage: Temporary browser memory may be used while the app is running to operate interactive features.
  • Authentication storage: The app stores non-password account metadata locally after sign-in so it can associate the device with the authenticated STOCKCRASH account. Email passwords are not stored in local storage.
  • Push-notification storage: If you voluntarily enable push alerts, the app may store notification preferences and identifiers needed to provide that feature.

3. Why these technologies are used

They are used to provide requested app functionality, remember your choices, maintain your simulator experience, support optional features and improve reliability and security. STOCKCRASH does not currently use these technologies to sell personal information or to build an advertising profile.

4. Third-party services

Some STOCKCRASH features communicate with third-party services, such as market-data, news, hosting or notification providers. Those services may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you interact directly with their systems. Their practices are governed by their respective policies. STOCKCRASH does not intentionally place third-party advertising cookies in the current app.

5. Managing or deleting stored data

You can remove locally stored STOCKCRASH information using the app's available delete/reset controls, by clearing the app's/site's storage, or by uninstalling the app. Blocking browser storage may cause some STOCKCRASH features to lose saved preferences or simulator history.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if STOCKCRASH introduces analytics, advertising, authentication, payments or other technologies that change how cookies or similar technologies are used. Material changes will be reflected in the policy and, where appropriate, presented to users in the app.

7. Contact

For privacy or cookie questions, use the STOCKCRASH privacy request mechanism.

Important: This policy is intended to provide clear disclosure and support Google Play privacy transparency. It should be reviewed against the exact production build, SDKs, hosting configuration and third-party services before public launch.
